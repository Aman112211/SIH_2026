# Architecture

A Flask application factory initializes SQLAlchemy and registers a single presentation blueprint. Models provide the universal product and government intelligence data shape. Deterministic services calculate listing health, wage-aware pricing, production recommendations and intervention priorities. Templates are server-rendered; the small JavaScript layer only enhances voice/demo interactions.

The external AI boundary is intentionally kept separate from calculations. A future provider can implement transcription, extraction and natural-language explanation without becoming the source of financial or business facts.
